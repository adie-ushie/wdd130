Calabar Rapids Rafting

At Calabar Rapids Rafting, our mission is to provide exciting, safe, and memorable white water rafting experiences. We believe adventure brings people together and creates memories that last a lifetime. Our experienced guides are committed to helping every guest enjoy the beauty and excitement of the river.

History

Calabar Rapids Rafting began with a group of outdoor enthusiasts who shared a passion for adventure and teamwork. Their dream was to create unforgettable rafting experiences while encouraging respect for nature and the environment.

Over the years, the company has grown into a trusted rafting destination for families, friends, and adventure seekers. We continue to provide safe and exciting trips while preserving the natural beauty of our rivers for future generations.
