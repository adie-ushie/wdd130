Calabar Rapids Rafting

At Calabar Rapids Rafting, our mission is to provide exciting, safe, and memorable white water rafting experiences. We believe that adventure brings people together and creates memories that last a lifetime. Our friendly team is committed to helping every visitor enjoy the beauty and excitement of the river.

History

Calabar Rapids Rafting was created with a passion for adventure, teamwork, and the great outdoors. What started as a small group of rafting enthusiasts has grown into a company dedicated to providing enjoyable river experiences for families, friends, and visitors. Our goal is to continue creating exciting adventures while respecting and protecting the natural environment.
